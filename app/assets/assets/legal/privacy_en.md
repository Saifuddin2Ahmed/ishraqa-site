# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This policy explains how we handle your data in **Ishraqa**. Your privacy is a trust we take seriously: we collect only what the app needs to work, never sell your data, and show no ads.

## In short
- You can read the daily wisdom, articles and library **without an account**; your preferences stay on your device.
- An account is needed to take part: clubs, comments, "Your Culture", "Sada", challenges and following.
- **No ads, no data selling, no advertising ID.**
- What you post is reviewed to protect the community, sometimes with the help of an AI tool that **does not use your data to train its models**.
- You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

## 1. What stays on your device
Stored **on your device only** and never sent to our servers: favorites, saved articles, your place and bookmarks in books, streaks, points and badges, interests, theme, text size and local notification settings, and books you download for offline reading.

## 2. Your account
- **Email address:** to sign you in with a one-time code, or through your Google account if you choose. Never used for marketing.
- **Name and country:** we ask for your full name and country when you create an account, and you may add your region or state. We use country and region to place you in your country's community for reading challenges and to show opportunities and features that fit where you live.
- **Your public profile:** display name, photo, bio, region and country, social links if you add them, and a verified badge if you earn one. These are **visible to other app users**.

## 3. What you post and share
- **"Your Culture" posts** and their images, and **comments** on articles, books and posts.
- **Club messages:** discussions, quotes, polls, voice notes, events and your attendance. Visible to club members, and in public clubs possibly to others.
- **"Sada":** your voice recordings (stories, proverbs, poetry, voice replies) with their title, description and region. If you record an elder in your family, you confirm they agreed to publication; we store the time of that confirmation, and we do not ask for the speaker's name, only a general description you choose.
- **Gifts, sponsorships and pledges:** the gifted book and your message, club sponsorship offers, and the pledge details and contact method you write in it.
- **"Contact us" messages:** your name if you give it, your email so we can reply, and your message.

**Microphone** permission is requested only when you tap record; nothing is recorded in the background. **Photos** permission is requested only when you pick an image.

## 4. Your activity in the app
If you are signed in, we store on our servers:
- **Reading days and duration** (not what you read), to track your goal, your club's streak and community challenges. Clubs see totals, not who read.
- **Days you open the app**, to celebrate milestones such as "Day 10 with Ishraqa".
- **Your interactions:** likes, follows, listens, club memberships, awards, reports and block lists.
- **Moderation records:** when a post is edited, deleted or hidden we keep a short record to protect the community and resolve disputes.

## 5. Notifications
- Morning, reading and deadline reminders are **scheduled on your device**.
- Daily-article, club and account-activity notifications are sent through a notification service using a **device notification token**, linked to your account only when signed in and removed when you sign out.
- Control each type in Settings, or turn them off in your phone settings. Your notifications are also kept in an in-app inbox.

## 6. Analytics and crash reports
We use **Google Analytics for Firebase** for general usage analytics (screens and events), and a crash reporting service that includes technical device and system information when a crash happens, to improve the app. Neither includes your name or email, and **the advertising ID is never collected**. Learn more about [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 7. Artificial intelligence
We use an AI provider for specific tasks:
- **Reviewing content before publication:** the text of "Your Culture" posts and the titles and descriptions of "Sada" recordings, to detect abuse and violations. Our review team makes the final call in unclear cases.
- **"Remind me where I was", explanations and discussion questions:** only the text of books and quotes is sent, never your personal data.
- **The "Club Spirit" award:** challenge discussion messages are sent **with anonymous codes instead of names and accounts**, so the content is judged, not the people.

We never send the provider your email or name. **The provider does not use what we send to train its models**, and keeps it only for a limited period for safety purposes before deleting it.

## 8. How we use data
Only to run the app: sign-in, content and interactions, clubs and challenges, notifications, content review and reports, protecting the community from abuse and fake accounts, and improving the app. **We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.**

## 9. Who processes data with us
We rely on a small number of trusted service providers; they process data on our behalf, only under our instructions, and are bound to protect it:

- **A hosting and database provider:** database, files, recordings and sign-in, on secure servers in the European Union.
- **A notification service and a crash reporting service, and Google Analytics for Firebase** for analytics.
- **An AI provider that does not use data to train its models:** for the tasks in section 7.
- **An email service:** only to send your sign-in code.
- **Payments:** optional support is processed by Google Play; we never see your card details.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

## 10. Retention and deletion
- Account data is kept while your account exists.
- **Things you delete yourself**, such as comments and club messages, move to "Deleted items" where you can restore them for 30 days; after that they are permanently deleted with their files automatically.
- **Delete your account:** **Account → Delete account**. Your account, public profile, posts, comments, messages, recordings, images, activity and notifications are deleted, and their files are removed from storage automatically. Encrypted backups may persist for up to 30 days, then are deleted.
- You can also request deletion through our [contact page](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/contact/); we act within 30 days. See [Delete account](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/delete-account/).

## 11. Your rights
You may access, correct (via "Edit profile") and delete your data, object to processing, or request a copy through our [contact page](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/contact/).

## 12. Children
The app is for a general audience. **Accounts are for users aged 13 and over.** We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; tell us and we will delete such an account.

## 13. Security
Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database access rules prevent any user from viewing another user's private data or modifying it. We review and test these rules continuously.

## 14. Changes
If we make material changes we will notify you in the app; the last-updated date appears at the top.

## Who we are and how to reach us
We are **OtwoX Digital Solutions**, the team that builds Ishraqa and looks after your data. For any question or request about your privacy or account, [contact us](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/contact/) or use **Account → Contact us** in the app; we reply within 30 days at the latest.
