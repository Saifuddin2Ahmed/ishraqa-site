# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how we handle your data in **Ishraqa**. We collect as little data as possible, never sell it, and show no ads.

## In short
- You can use almost all of the app **without an account**; your preferences stay on your device.
- An account is optional and only needed to write, comment and follow.
- **No ads, no data selling, no advertising ID.**
- You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

## 1. Without an account
Stored **on your device only** and never sent to our servers: favorites, saved articles, streaks, points and badges, reading goal and book progress, greeting name, interests, theme and notification settings.

## 2. When you create an account
- **Email address:** to sign you in with a one-time code. Never used for marketing.
- **Your public profile:** display name (a pseudonym is fine), photo, bio, region and country, and social links if you add them. These are **publicly visible**.
- **What you post:** "Your Culture" posts and images, comments, voice recordings in "Grandma's Wisdom", and articles if you are an Ishraqa writer. Shown publicly under your display name, some after review.
- **Your activity:** likes, follows, reports and block lists.
- **Notification preference** and your in-app notification inbox.

**Microphone** permission is requested only when you tap record. Nothing is recorded in the background.

## 3. Notifications
- Morning, reading and deadline reminders are **scheduled on your device**.
- Daily-article, Ishraqa news and account-activity notifications are sent via Firebase using a **device notification token**, linked to your account only when signed in and removed when you sign out.
- Control each type in Settings, or turn them off in your phone settings.

## 4. Analytics and crash reports
We use Google Firebase for **anonymous** usage analytics (screens and events) and crash reports to improve the app. They are not linked to your name or email, and **we removed the advertising ID from the app**, so it is never collected. Article read counts are aggregate counters that do not record who read.

## 5. How we use data
Only to run the app: sign-in, content and interactions, follows, notifications, content review and reports, and protecting the community from abuse. **We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.**

## 6. Who processes data with us
We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. We rely on a small number of trusted service providers to run the app; they process data on our behalf, only under our instructions, and are bound to protect it:

- **Cloud hosting:** database, file storage and sign-in, on secure servers in the European Union.
- **Notifications, analytics and crash reports:** through Google Firebase, including Google Analytics for Firebase. Learn more about [how Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- **Email delivery:** only to send your sign-in code.
- **Payments:** optional support is processed by Google Play; we never see your card details.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users.

## 7. Retention and deletion
- Account data is kept while your account exists.
- **Delete your account:** **Account → Delete account**. Your account, public profile, posts, comments, likes, follows, recordings, images and notifications are deleted immediately. Encrypted backups may persist for up to 30 days, then are deleted automatically.
- You can also request deletion through our [contact page](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/contact/); we act within 30 days. See [Delete account](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/delete-account/).

## 8. Your rights
You may access, correct (via "Edit profile") and delete your data, object to processing, or request a copy through our [contact page](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/contact/).

## 9. Children
The app is for a general audience. **Accounts are for users aged 13 and over.** We do not knowingly collect data from younger children; tell us and we will delete such an account.

## 10. Security
Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database access rules prevent any user from viewing another user's private data or modifying it. We review and test these rules continuously.

## 11. Changes
If we make material changes we will notify you in the app; the last-updated date appears at the top.

## Who we are and how to reach us
We are **OtwoX Digital Solutions**, the team that builds Ishraqa and looks after your data. For any question or request about your privacy or account, [contact us](https://ishraqa.otwox.com/en/contact/) or use **Account → Contact us** in the app; we reply within 30 days at the latest.
