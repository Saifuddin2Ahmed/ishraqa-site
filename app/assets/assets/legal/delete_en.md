# Delete your account and data

You can delete your **Ishraqa** account and all data linked to it at any time, in one of two ways:

## In the app
1. Open Ishraqa and sign in.
2. Go to **Account**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, then confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted within moments and cannot be recovered afterwards.

## If the app is no longer on your phone
Send a request through our [contact page](https://ishraqa.app/en/contact/?topic=delete_account), choosing "Delete my account" and using the email registered to your account. We will verify it, delete the account within 30 days, and confirm by email.

## Delete some data without deleting your account
- **Your posts, comments, replies and club messages:** delete any of them yourself. They move to "Deleted items" in your account and are erased permanently after 30 days (or immediately if you delete them there).
- **Your reactions:** remove any like or reaction at any time.
- **Your "Echo" recordings and covers, and "My highlights":** delete any of them at any time.
- **Your photo, bio and links:** edit or remove them from Account → Edit profile. A previous username is deleted automatically 14 days after you change it.
- **Your memberships:** leave any club, community or Space and your membership is removed immediately. You can also withdraw a sponsorship request until it is assigned, a pending mentorship request, and a competition entry while the competition is open.
- **Registered devices:** remove any device from Settings → Registered devices.
- **Any other data:** ask us through our [contact page](https://ishraqa.app/en/contact/?topic=privacy) ("Privacy and my data") with the email on your account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted
- Your account and email address
- Your public profile: name, photo, bio and links
- "Your Culture" posts and images, comments and replies, likes, reactions and mentions
- Your voice recordings in "Echo" and their covers, and voice notes in clubs
- Your class homework with its recordings, attachments and voice feedback, plus your grades, attendance and certificates
- Reading sync and "My highlights"
- Your memberships, competition entries and votes, developer-club stand-ups and mentorship requests
- Follows, reports and block lists
- Notification tokens and your notification inbox

## What may remain
- Encrypted backups for up to **30 days**, then deleted automatically.
- If you own a Space or club, ownership passes to its admin or moderator, otherwise to the longest-standing member; a Space with no other members is deleted with its files.
- Space files and posts you uploaded as an admin, and developer-club tasks, remain with the Space or team **without your name**.
- Sponsorship requests: contact details, message and logo are erased, an individual's name is replaced with "Former sponsor", and unassigned requests are withdrawn; the record of an assigned sponsorship stays with our team.
- Support conversations: kept **without your name or email** and unlinked from your account for up to **one year**, then deleted.
- Anonymous aggregate statistics not linked to you, such as article read counts.
- Data stored only on your device, such as favorites and mood notes, which is removed when you uninstall the app.
