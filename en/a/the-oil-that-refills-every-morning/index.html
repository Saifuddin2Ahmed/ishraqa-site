Why Data Is Not the New Oil

We were told data is the new oil. But oil lies under the ground, and attention lies inside you. Oil runs out; attention refills every night while you sleep. So the new war isn't over fields. It's over your hours, starting with the first one of the morning.

Saifuddin Ahmed8 min read · 2026-11-04

Let me ask you a small question. What was the first thing you did this morning, after you opened your eyes?

If you're like most people, you reached for your phone before you washed your face. Messages. Notifications. A short clip. Then another. And in those first minutes, when your mind was at its clearest, a lot of parties were competing for one thing you own.

In May 2017 The Economist ran a headline that became the slogan of a decade: the world's most valuable resource is no longer oil, but data. The idea I want to put to you is that this comparison is wrong, and that its mistake is exactly what we need to understand. Data isn't the new oil. It's more dangerous, because the well is inside you, and it refills every night while you sleep.
