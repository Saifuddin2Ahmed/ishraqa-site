Developer Clubs:
Build what lasts

A small team, an expert mentor, and an open-source project the world can see.


    

  





  
Why Developer Clubs?
Because talent is distributed equally, but opportunities are not.
🧭
Expert Mentor
You learn from those who came before you: a review of your code, guidance on your decisions, and an answer when you get stuck.
🛠️
A project that gets used
No isolated exercises; you build something people use.
🌍
Open source
Everything you build stays yours and the world's, in a public repository open to everyone.
🏆
A Visible Journey
Your project in the Projects Showcase under your team's name, ready to add to your résumé and share.
👥
A Team Like You
Peers from your city, your university, or anywhere else, brought together by one passion.
🌿
No ads
A calm space where you focus on what you're building, like everything in Ishraqa.

  
How It Works
From idea to Projects Showcase in five steps.
Join or createDownload Ishraqa and join an existing developers' club or create your own.
Team and mentorUp to seven members, and the mentor writes the project idea and breaks it down into small tasks.
A meeting each weekA club event on the platform of your choice.
DeliveryA public repository link, which the mentor reviews and either approves or asks for changes.
GalleryThe project appears in the Projects Showcase for everyone to see.

  
  
Become a Mentor
If you have experience to share, mentoring in Ishraqa is your path to making an impact that lasts: a team you lead, a project you're proud of, and the Mentor badge on your profile. Contact Us and choose “Partnership or collaboration,” or create a developers' club directly from the app.
