Support Ishraqa

Knowledge doesn't grow through one person's effort, and it shouldn't stop at the edge of those who hold it.

Ishraqa began with a simple idea: that reading can open a new door for a person, and that knowledge, once shared, reaches further than we imagine.

From a small space for reading and conversation, a journey began to build a place that brings knowledge, learning and expression together, giving readers a chance to discover, learners a chance to grow, and programmers a chance to make something new.

But ideas, however valuable, need someone to tend them before they can grow. They need technical foundations that last, content that keeps renewing, development that never stops, and human effort that turns an idea into an experience people can benefit from.

Ishraqa is free, and it will stay free, because we believe access to knowledge shouldn't depend on the ability to pay.

So your support is not the price of using Ishraqa. It is a contribution to keeping it going and growing, enriching the knowledge it offers, and widening what it can open up for readers, learners and creators.

Where does your support go?

To keeping knowledge within reach.

  • Infrastructure and hosting: to keep Ishraqa running and its content available to the people who use it.

  • Content and knowledge: to enrich the experience with material worth reading and discovering, opening new horizons for learning.

  • Creators and contributors: to recognize the effort writers and creators put into enriching the content, whenever resources allow.

  • Development and innovation: to improve the experience and build tools that make reading, learning and expression easier.

  • Keeping the project going: to provide the resources, time and effort needed to keep building and developing Ishraqa.

We are not here to ask you to pay for knowledge, but to open a way for you to help make it available.

What we plant in our minds today may tomorrow become an idea someone develops, a solution someone else builds, or knowledge passed from one generation to the next.

Ishraqa is a growing project, and every sincere contribution can become part of its journey.

Help enrich knowledge, and be part of what we can build together.
